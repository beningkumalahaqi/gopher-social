How this queue is built

Quiz evidence

The best attempt per quiz is compared against a 70 percent pass line. Below that, the lesson is added.

Reading evidence

A lesson opened more than seven days ago and still marked in progress is added.

No invented scores

There is no model of what you probably know. The queue is derived from stored attempts and timestamps.
